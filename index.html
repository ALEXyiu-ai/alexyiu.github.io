About Alex

Alex Yiu is a Generative AI Consultant and Creative Technology Professional with 26+ years of experience in design, eLearning, digital innovation and AI-enabled content creation.

Graduated from the School of Design at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Alex combines visual communication, learning technology and generative AI to help organisations unlock new possibilities in creativity, education and innovation.

His recent focus is on practical Generative AI training, AI storytelling, AI reel production, AI video generation, prompt engineering and creative media workflows for schools, NGOs and corporate teams.

Positioning: Not just teaching AI tools, but guiding participants through the full creative journey from idea, prompt, storyboard, image generation and video creation to final AI-powered reels and trailers.
